# Admonition Alerts

> [!NOTE]
> Useful information the reader should know, tinted blue.

> [!TIP]
> A helpful suggestion, tinted green.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Key information that needs attention, tinted purple.

> [!WARNING]
> Urgent info that needs immediate attention, tinted orange.

> [!CAUTION]
> Advises about risks or negative outcomes, tinted red.

> [!NOTE] Same-line remainder flows after the label.
> Continuation lines keep the tinted bar.

> A plain blockquote keeps the default bar — only `> [!KIND]`
> leaders recolor, and `:::note` containers stay literal text.

:::note
This container form is out of scope and renders literally.
:::
